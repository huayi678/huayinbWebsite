At huayinb (“we”, “our”, or “us”), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our games or services. Please read this policy carefully.

01 Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: When you use our games or services, we may collect information such as your username, email address, and other identifiable details.
  • Device Information: We may collect information about your device, including device type, operating system, IP address, and other technical details.
  • Usage Data: We collect data on your interaction with the game, such as progress, scores, and other in-game activity.
  • Location Information: With your consent, we may collect location data to provide location-based services.

02 How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our services, including bug fixing and customer support.
  • To analyze game data to improve user experience.
  • To personalize in-game content and display relevant ads.
  • To prevent fraud and illegal activity.

03 Information Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except in the following situations:

  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law or regulation.
  • Third-Party Service Providers: We may share your information with trusted third-party partners (e.g., ad networks and analytics providers) to improve the game experience.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your information may be transferred as part of the business assets.

Sale or Sharing of Personal Information for Advertising: By accepting this Privacy Policy and using our games or services, you acknowledge and, where permitted by applicable law, consent to our sale or sharing of certain personal information, including device identifiers, usage data, and advertising information, with advertising, analytics, and measurement partners for personalized advertising, analytics, and related business purposes. These disclosures may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information under applicable privacy laws, even when no money is exchanged. You may withdraw your consent or opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, targeted advertising, or profiling at any time using the privacy controls available in our services or your device settings. Contact details will be published in the Contact Us section when available. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

04 Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

05 Data Retention

We will retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. After that, the information will be deleted or anonymized.

06 Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that we have collected personal information from a child, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

07 Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You also have the right to withdraw consent and opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information, personalized or targeted advertising, profiling, and analytics services. You can use the privacy controls available in our services or your device settings. Contact details will be published in the Contact Us section when available.

08 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you in advance.
